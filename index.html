ABOUT PURSAT

A Province Full of Natural Beauty

Pursat Province is located in western Cambodia. Pursat town lies along the Pursat River, while the wider province extends toward the Tonlé Sap and Cardamom Mountains.

The province combines riverside life, countryside scenery, cultural sites and natural destinations.
